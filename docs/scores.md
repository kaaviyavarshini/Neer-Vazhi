Depth error is the mean absolute difference between the model's deepest water within one cell and the reported depth, over the 71 points that have a reported depth.

Generated by `node scripts/score_presets.mjs`. This is a visualisation score, not an engineering validation.

## Road burning: before and after (michaung_2023, 258 mm)

| road burn | hit rate | wet area | Very High/High flooded | Very Low flooded |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 0 m (off) | 37% | 15.8% | 36% | 12% |
| 0.5 m | 44% | 16.0% | 35% | 13% |
| 1 m (used) | 50% | 16.1% | 34% | 14% |
| 2 m | 57% | 15.0% | 30% | 13% |

Burning helps the known-spot hit rate but slightly worsens the flood-zone map score, so the evidence is mixed. All four values were looked at, so 1 m is not a clean untuned choice. The "road under water" figure on the page rises from about 65 km to about 155 km because roads are now the low channels.
