OMR + Pallikaranai

Between the elevated IT corridor of OMR and the low-lying Pallikaranai freshwater marshland, monsoonal runoff naturally drains westward into the marsh basin. Urban infill across Perungudi, Thoraipakkam, and Sholinganallur constricts natural drainage channels, causing severe surface pooling along arterial roads.
